Three rules. One: release, or any fault, and it stops and brakes at once. Two: it moves down only while someone holds "Down". Sitting still for long only gives a reminder and calls family; it never lowers by itself, because a weak person slips off a moving seat more easily. Three: after you stand, the seat stays put, so it can still catch you. To return to bed, the empty seat stops at 0.50 metres; once seated, hold "Down" to reach the bed. Note: release-to-stop is a user command. Lock-out is the sensor self-check: forces 15 to 35% off body weight mean slow down and warn; above 35% for half a second, it locks. Legs giving way is different: the speed rule stops the seat to catch you.
