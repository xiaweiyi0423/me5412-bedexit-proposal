OUTLINE

Five parts, from the problem to results

1

Problem & users

Why rising at night is hard; who it is for; design requirements

2

Concept

Existing solutions; other ways to rise compared: effort, and catching the user if legs give way

3

Mechanism & stability

Path → mechanism → sizing; motion demo; frame stability; sideways falls

4

Control & safety

Sensing and speed; state machine; six risks and their guards

5

Comparison & wrap‑up

Products compared; three advantages; limits → next version; conclusion

17 main slides + 46 s film, about 15 min. In the appendix: use flow, cantilever loads, speed simulation, fall detection off the footplate, FEA plan

The talk has five parts: the problem and users, a comparison of solutions, the mechanism, then control and safety. We finish with results and a short wrap-up.
