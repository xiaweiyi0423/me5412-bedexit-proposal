These are the four steps from the film, with the CAD parts driven by the real kinematics: slide onto the seat, grip the switch, rise together, release to stop. The seat moves forward, up, and tilts. The tilt peaks at about 16 degrees, and is about 13 at seat-off. Bottom right is the 2D model. Red is the cushion contact: it supports the hips first, then the thighs, so the user does not slide forward.
