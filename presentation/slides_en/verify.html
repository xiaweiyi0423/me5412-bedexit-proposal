From the 2D quasi-static STS model (81 users) and control simulation; strength by hand calculation. No hardware or FEA yet: see next page

Now the results. All six requirements are met in our models and simulation. Rising takes one third less effort than from a low bed, like a raised bed, but the bed stays low. For 80 of 81 users, the seat follows the thighs and catches a sit-back. If the legs give way, the seat catches the hips the whole way. A typical user is up in about 7 seconds, and the seat stops on release. Fixed to the bed, the frame does not tip. All 16 scenarios end safe. Next: what is not done yet.
