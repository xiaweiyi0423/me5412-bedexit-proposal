This table maps the six requirements from the problem slide to how we checked them. Effort: 33% less than a low bed, about the same as a raised bed. Seat follows the user: for 80 of 81 users, there is no forward slide, and a sit-back is caught. A vertical lift manages only 11. If the legs give way, the hips drop straight down, and the seat catches them through the whole rise. The push limit, release to stop, speed that follows the user, and down only while held all pass in state-machine simulation. Still in progress: stability with real masses, and FEA for strength and cantilever deflection.
