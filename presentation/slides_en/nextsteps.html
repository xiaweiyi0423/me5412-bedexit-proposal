Modelling and FEA: start with these four areas (weak spots from hand calculations, not FEA results)

① Carriage plate + bearing housing weldment

586 N·m enters the carriage plate via the bearing housing; check the housing root weld and both sides of the 80° window

② Seat beam + gusset plate weld

Hand calc 113 MPa (allowable 237); check the root weld and outer-edge sag (hand calc 1.6 mm)

③ Housing side plate (at the vertical slot)

Carriage forces go into the side plate; the slot weakens the section: check slot-end stress and housing twist

④ Grab-bar post + base

1668 N at the bar end, root moment 1.53 kN·m; check the post root weld and base deformation

Load vectors, boundary conditions and criteria for each area are in the “FEA focus” section of the mechanism web page; CAD parts and STEP files can be downloaded there

Next, we hand over to our modelling and FEA members. Hand calculations found four areas to analyse first. Note: these are not FEA results yet. One: the carriage plate and bearing housing weldment. The seat moment enters the carriage plate here, so we check the weld at the housing root and both sides of the 80 degree window. Two: the weld between the seat beam and the gusset plate. The hand calculation gives 113 megapascals; we check the weld and the edge sag. Three: the housing side plate. It has a vertical slot for the arm, so we check stress at the slot ends and twist of the whole housing. Four: the grab-bar post and base. When a user hangs fully on the bar, the root moment is about 1.5 kilonewton-metres. Loads, boundary conditions and criteria are on the mechanism web page, with the part models and STEP files.
