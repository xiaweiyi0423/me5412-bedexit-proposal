Step two: we compared five mechanisms, with the same constraints and the same 81 users. A vertical lift cannot follow the thigh. A four-bar under the seat hits the shins. Outside both legs, it blocks the way back to bed and needs two synchronised actuators. On the bed-head side only, it cannot carry the offset load: the ground bearing sees about 25 kilonewtons. The inclined guide with a tilt link is off by only 8 millimetres, needs the least force, and keeps the foot end open. The cost: a tall mechanism housing.
