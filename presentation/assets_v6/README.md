# Deck v6 (15-minute version) assets

- `icons/*.svg`: side-view pictograms drawn by `make_icons.py` (no text inside; labels are slide text). `opt_*` are the four ways to rise on the comparison page (same moment: hip just off the support); `prior_*` are the existing products on the prior-art page. Schematic, not to scale; orange = the moving part.
- `clip_motion.mp4` (film 0:29.5–0:36, the four steps) and `clip_sensing.mp4` (film 0:36–0:39.2, the four sensors), silent loops rendered from the pulse film frames; `still_*.jpg` are their poster frames. The full film is `presentation/video/pulse/bedexit_pulse.mp4`.
- Uploaded blob ids are in `presentation/animations/blobs.json`.
- `presentation/tools/render_thumbs_v6.js` renders slides with blobs mapped to local files (thumbnails for the talk guide); `presentation/tools/patch_guide_v6.py` patches the guide.
